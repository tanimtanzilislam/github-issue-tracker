I joined Founder Institute as a founder. Then I went through Funding Lab. Now, I am fortunate to be part of the team supporting the next generation of founders. That journey has changed how I think about building companies, especially at a time when AI is changing what one person or a small team can achieve. Today, you do not need a large organization to start building something meaningful. With the right use of AI, a strong idea, and a structured support system, it is possible to move faster, test earlier, and build with far fewer limitations than before. But using AI tools is not the same as becoming an AI-native founder. It requires a different way of thinking about the product, operations, team, and business model from the beginning. This is why I believe the upcoming Founder Institute Graduate Program is relevant for people who are already building, working inside companies, or simply carrying an idea they have not yet acted on. The future will favour founders who understand how to build with AI, not just add AI later. The early admissions fee deadline is June 30, 2026.
